Owl achievement · 2025–26

State Final Four

Olmsted’s boys basketball team captured the Section VI Class B1 title, beating Medina 68–33, then won the overall Class B championship over Tapestry, 58–45. The Owls advanced to the NYSPHSAA state semifinals in Binghamton, finishing among New York’s final four Class B teams.

Senior Tacari led the Class B crossover final with 36 points. Congratulations to Coach Bush and the Owls on an outstanding season.
